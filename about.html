About ASCEND

Our mission and background

ASCEND Academy is a nonprofit organization that supports younger and underrepresented students by providing free instruction in PSAT preparation and machine learning.

RA

Rohan Arunkumar

Founder and Executive Director

Rohan is passionate about machine learning research and using technology to solve real-world problems. Driven by a commitment to education and service, he founded ASCEND Academy to expand access to high-quality academic support for students. In addition to leading ASCEND Academy, Rohan serves as a Senior Tutor on Schoolhouse.world, where he teaches math and programming to learners around the world. He enjoys giving back to his community through mentorship and service initiatives that help students reach their full potential.

About the Organization

What ASCEND does

ASCEND Academy provides instruction in PSAT 8/9/10 Math and English, test-taking strategies, STEM topics, and computer science concepts such as machine learning. All programs are offered free of charge to enrolled students.

The program has piloted with two PSAT English and Math courses, impacting over 100 children across Jersey City. An additional 35 students are being served through a new PSAT course and a machine learning course, both offered in a 1-on-1 format.

Our Approach

How we work

ASCEND Academy is built around the idea that access to quality test preparation and technology education should not depend on a student's financial background. Programs are led by volunteer instructors and structured to meet students where they are, regardless of prior experience.

Instruction is kept small in scale to allow for more individualized support. The current machine learning course is offered as 1-on-1 instruction for this reason.

Core Values

What guides our programs

01

Free access

All programs are offered at no cost to students. There are no fees for enrollment, materials, or instruction.

02

Individualized instruction

Programs are structured to give students meaningful attention, whether in small cohorts or 1-on-1 sessions.

03

Practical skills

Content is focused on skills that directly benefit students, from standardized test performance to foundational computer science knowledge.
