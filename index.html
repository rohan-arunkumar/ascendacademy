About ASCEND Academy

ASCEND Academy is a nonprofit organization that supports younger and underrepresented students by providing instruction in PSAT 8/9/10 Math and English, test-taking strategies, STEM topics, and computer science concepts such as machine learning.

The program has piloted with two PSAT English and Math courses, impacting over 100 children across Jersey City. An additional 35 students are being reached through a new PSAT course and a machine learning course, both offered as 1-on-1 instruction.

Learn More About Us

PSAT 8/9/10 Preparation

Structured instruction in Math and English with a focus on test-taking strategy, pacing, and content review. Courses are delivered over 1 month across multiple sessions in a group cohort format.

Machine Learning Foundations

An introduction to computer science concepts and machine learning for students with no prior coding experience. Currently offered as 1-on-1 instruction.
