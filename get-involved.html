For Students

Student Application

ASCEND Academy is open to students in 8th through 10th grade who are interested in PSAT preparation, machine learning, or both. Applications are reviewed on a rolling basis.

  • No application fee
  • No prior experience required for either program
  • Rolling admissions, open year-round

For Volunteers

Volunteer with ASCEND

ASCEND Academy relies on volunteer instructors and mentors to deliver its programs. Volunteers may teach PSAT preparation, lead machine learning instruction, or provide general tutoring support.

  • Flexible time commitment
  • Curriculum and materials provided
  • In-person and virtual opportunities available
ASCEND Academy
