Our Programs

ASCEND Academy currently offers two programs: PSAT 8/9/10 preparation and a machine learning foundations course. Both are provided at no cost.

Program 01

PSAT 8/9/10 Preparation

The PSAT preparation program provides instruction in both the Math and English sections of the PSAT 8/9 and PSAT 10. Students work through content review and test-taking strategy with the goal of building familiarity with the exam format and improving their scores over the course of the program. Each course is delivered over 1 month across multiple sessions. An additional cohort is currently underway in a 1-on-1 format.

  • PSAT Math: algebra, data analysis, and problem solving
  • PSAT English: reading comprehension and writing conventions
  • Test-taking strategies and time management
  • Practice exercises and progress tracking
Program 02

Machine Learning Foundations

The machine learning course introduces students to core computer science concepts and the fundamentals of machine learning. No prior coding experience is required. The course is currently offered as 1-on-1 instruction and is designed for students who are new to programming and AI.

  • Introduction to programming concepts
  • Overview of how machine learning works
  • Hands-on exercises with real data
  • Discussion of AI applications and ethics
Program Details

Format and eligibility

Duration

Each course runs over 1 month across multiple sessions. The machine learning course length may vary depending on the student.

Format

Group instruction for PSAT prep cohorts. The machine learning course is currently offered as 1-on-1 instruction.

Cost

Both programs are completely free for all enrolled students. There are no fees for materials or instruction.
