Contact

Get in touch with ASCEND Academy.

For any questions, including student enrollment, volunteering, or general inquiries, email us directly at ascend.academynj@gmail.com. We typically respond within 2 to 3 business days.

Students and Families

Applying to a program

If you are a student or parent interested in the PSAT preparation program or the machine learning course, send an email with your name, grade level, and which program you are interested in. We will follow up with availability and next steps.

Email Us to Apply
Tutors and Volunteers

Joining as a volunteer

If you are interested in volunteering as an instructor or mentor, email us with a brief description of your background and availability. We welcome tutors with experience in standardized test prep, computer science, or related fields. Both in-person and virtual opportunities are available.
